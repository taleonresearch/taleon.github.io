TalSign Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

TalSign (com.signageon.tv) is a digital signage application operated by Taleon Research and Development Private Limited, referred to below as “we”, “us”, or “our”. It enables authorised users to manage and display advertisements and other content on connected screens.

This policy covers the TalSign TV application and its associated dashboard and backend services.

Information We Collect

Account information: When an administrator or authorised user has a dashboard account, we store their name, email address, securely hashed password, access role, and assigned district where applicable. The TV application connects through a device pairing code rather than requiring a personal account on the TV.

Screen and device information: We process screen names, device identifiers, pairing credentials, notification tokens, app version, available storage, connection status, and last connection or synchronisation times. Device records may also include Android version, screen dimensions, and administrator-entered location or notes.

Content and business information: We store uploaded images, videos, content links, playlists, and scheduling settings. Where entered into the dashboard, we also process advertiser or store details, advertisement charges, and payment records.

Technical information: Our servers and infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, request information, and operational logs when devices connect to the service.

Screen locations are entered by authorised users; they are not a statement that the TV application tracks GPS location.

How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Authenticate users and pair authorised screens.
  • Deliver, schedule, and display signage content.
  • Synchronise content and report screen connectivity.
  • Send content-update notifications.
  • Manage user permissions and business records.
  • Maintain security, diagnose problems, and provide support.

Service Providers and Sharing

We use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to send update notifications to registered devices. This involves processing installation identifiers and messaging tokens. See Firebase's privacy information.

We use Cloudflare to provide connectivity and protect access to our services. Cloudflare may process network information, including IP addresses and request metadata. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Information is accessible to authorised organisation users according to their permissions. We may also disclose information where required by law.

We do not sell personal information or use it to build personalised advertising profiles.

Storage and Security

Account information, device records, and uploaded content are stored on the backend infrastructure used to operate TalSign.

The TV application also stores configuration and downloaded content locally so it can continue displaying cached advertisements during a network interruption.

We use authentication, password hashing, and access controls to protect information. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain necessary business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

To request access, correction, or deletion of your information, email taleonresearch@gmail.com. Include your organisation name and the relevant account email or screen name. Do not send passwords or pairing credentials.

We may verify your identity and authority before acting on a request. Information that must be retained for legal or accounting purposes may be kept for the required period. Backup copies may remain until the applicable backup retention cycle ends.

Removing information from the server does not necessarily erase content already cached on an offline TV. Clearing the application's data or uninstalling it removes its locally stored configuration and content.

Children's Privacy

TalSign is intended for business signage management, not for children to create accounts or submit personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The current version and its updated date will be published on this page.
