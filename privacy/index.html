Website Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Taleon Research and Development Private Limited (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the website at taleon.in, including www.taleon.in. This policy explains how information is handled when you visit our website or contact us through it.

This policy covers the company website. The TalSign TV application, dashboard and backend services are covered separately by the TalSign Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Enquiries: When you submit our contact form, we receive your name, email address and message, together with your phone number and organisation if you provide them. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you include in that correspondence.

Website usage: We use Google Analytics to understand visits and interactions with our website. Depending on your browser settings and the service configuration, this may include pages viewed, referral sources, interaction events, browser and device information, approximate geographic location and cookie-based identifiers.

Technical information: Website hosting, network and security providers may process IP addresses, request URLs, browser information, access times and operational logs to deliver and protect the website.

Please avoid including passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive personal information in the contact form.

How We Use Information

  • Respond to enquiries and discuss requested products, projects or services.
  • Maintain records of our communications and business relationships.
  • Understand website usage and improve content and performance.
  • Protect the website, investigate technical issues and prevent abuse.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and resolve disputes.

Cookies, Analytics and Your Choices

The main website includes a Google Analytics tag, which can use cookies to distinguish visits and measure interactions. We do not intentionally send the contents of contact-form submissions to Google Analytics.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect third-party features. Google also provides a Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on for supported desktop browsers. This add-on does not disable other services, such as embedded YouTube videos or hosting logs.

Read more about Google Analytics data practices and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Service Providers and Third-Party Content

Our website uses the following services, which process information as needed to provide their functions:

  • Web3Forms: Our contact form submits the information you enter to Web3Forms so your enquiry can be delivered to us. See the Web3Forms Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics: Google processes website interaction information to provide usage reports. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube: Product videos are embedded from YouTube. Loading embedded videos may connect your browser to Google and transmit technical information; YouTube may use cookies or other identifiers according to its policies and your settings. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts: The main website loads fonts from Google servers. Your browser sends network request information, including your IP address, when fetching those fonts. See Google Fonts privacy information.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare: These services provide website hosting, delivery and network protection. They may process technical request information and operational metrics. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

We use email services to receive and respond to enquiries. Information may also be accessible to authorised personnel who need it to handle your request, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect legal rights.

We do not sell the personal information you provide through our contact form. Links to other websites lead to services governed by their own privacy policies.

Storage, Retention and Security

Enquiries and correspondence may be held in our business email and related records. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to requests, maintain relevant business records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Analytics and infrastructure records are also subject to the applicable service settings and provider retention practices.

We take reasonable steps to protect information and limit access to people who need it. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Our service providers may process information in countries outside your own, including outside India.

Your Requests and Choices

You can choose whether to submit an enquiry and whether to provide optional contact details. Without the information needed to respond, we may be unable to handle your request.

To request access, correction or deletion of information you have provided, or to raise a privacy concern, email taleonresearch@gmail.com. Include enough information to identify the relevant enquiry, such as the email address you used. Do not send passwords or sensitive identity documents unless specifically requested through an appropriate channel.

We may need to verify your identity before acting. We will consider requests in accordance with applicable law. Some records may need to be retained for legal or business obligations, and backup copies may remain until their retention cycle ends.

Children's Privacy

Our website is intended to provide information about our products and business services. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our website or information-handling practices change. The current version and its updated date will be published on this page.
